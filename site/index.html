Luck, or a blind spot? Real Madrid

Ties won against ties the model expected each club to win, for clubs with at least 8 ties. Most clubs sit close to what the model expected. One doesn't.

Real Madrid went through ties when the model expected . If the model were right, that would happen about once in tries. The model rates Real accurately in La Liga, but in Champions League knockout matches they take about a third of a point a match more than expected.

So part of Real Madrid's "luck" isn't luck. Either they lift their level for these matches, or something that matters here isn't visible in league results.
